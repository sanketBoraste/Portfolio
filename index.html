I'm Sanket, a full-stack developer who lives at the intersection of engineering and product design. For the past three years I've helped startups ship dashboards, storefronts and developer tools that people use every day.

My happy place is a blank repo, a clear problem and a tight feedback loop. I like owning features end to end — from sketching the flow in Figma, to modelling the data, to the last 2px of padding. When I'm not coding, I'm writing about CSS, mentoring juniors or losing a chess game online.

05 · How I work

From idea to shipped.

A simple, transparent process built around short feedback loops — no black boxes.

01

Discover

Understand the user, the constraint and the metric that matters. Write it down before touching code.

02

Design

Sketch the flow, prototype the risky parts and agree on the smallest lovable version.

03

Build

Small commits, accessible components, tests around the parts that would hurt to break.

04

Ship

Measure, learn, refine. Launch day is the start of the work, not the finish line.
